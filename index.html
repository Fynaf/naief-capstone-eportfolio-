Welcome to my capstone ePortfolio for the APPLHSCI 9099 Capstone Experience at Western University's Faculty of Health Sciences. I am a nephrologist and internist completing subspecialty fellowship training in glomerulonephritis and vasculitis at Western University, with a year-long quality improvement (QI) project embedded in the Glomerulonephritis (GN) Clinic at London Health Sciences Centre (LHSC).

My project focuses on integrating genetic testing into routine nephrology care — translating evidence from clinical genomics into practice at a real health system level. This portfolio documents my learning journey across the five AHS Key Competencies, demonstrating how interprofessional collaboration, evidence-based leadership, and systems thinking were applied throughout that work.
