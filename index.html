APPLHSCI 9099 Capstone Experience  ·  Western University  ·  2025–26

I am a nephrologist and internist, and I finished my glomerulonephritis and vasculitis fellowship at Western in June 2026.

For a year I worked on getting genetic testing ordered directly in the nephrology clinics at LHSC. The package was built and taken into clinic. Not one test was ordered. What stopped it was about nine minutes of out-of-province paperwork per patient that no clinician could hand off.

This portfolio is what I did, what I found, and what I would do differently.

LIVE CLINICAL TOOL
Kidney Genetic Testing Decision Support App

Determines patient eligibility for genetic testing and recommends the appropriate test type. Built and functioning; not formally validated.
