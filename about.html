About Me

I am a nephrologist and internist with over eight years of postgraduate clinical training across Saudi Arabia and Canada. I completed my internal medicine residency at King Faisal Specialist Hospital & Research Centre in Jeddah, followed by subspecialty fellowship training in adult nephrology and glomerulonephritis/vasculitis at Western University. I am currently completing an MSc in Clinical Epidemiology at Western University, with a focus on research design and evidence synthesis.

My clinical work spans general nephrology, glomerular disease, genetic kidney disease, and onco-nephrology. This ePortfolio documents my APPLHSCI 9099 Capstone Experience, a year-long quality improvement initiative embedded in the Glomerulonephritis Clinic at LHSC, supervised by Dr. Dervla Connaughton (Field Mentor, Assistant Professor of Medicine, Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry, Division of Nephrology) and Dr. Marcy Saxe-Braithwaite (Academic Lead, Western University).
