About Me

I am a nephrologist and internist with nearly eight years of postgraduate clinical training in Saudi Arabia and Canada. I completed internal medicine residency and nephrology training at King Faisal Specialist Hospital & Research Centre in Jeddah, then an adult nephrology fellowship and a glomerulonephritis and vasculitis fellowship at Western University. I am completing a Master of Clinical Science in Clinical Epidemiology and Research Management at Western, expected August 2026.

My clinical work spans general nephrology, glomerular disease, genetic kidney disease, obstetric nephrology and onco-nephrology. This ePortfolio documents my APPLHSCI 9099 Capstone Experience, a year-long quality improvement initiative in the general nephrology clinics at London Health Sciences Centre, with the Glomerulonephritis Clinic as the pilot site. It was supervised by Dr. Dervla Connaughton (Field Mentor, Division of Nephrology, Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry) and Dr. Marcy Saxe-Braithwaite (Academic Lead, Western University).
