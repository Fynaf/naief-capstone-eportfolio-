Project Deliverables

Digital Tools & Infrastructure
Digital Tool

Kidney Genetic Testing Clinical Decision Support App

Web-based tool that determines patient eligibility for genetic testing and recommends the appropriate test type. Clinical logic and rules engine designed by the student; built with an external development team. Built and functioning; checked internally against real cases by the student and a co-fellow; informally reviewed by the field mentor. Not formally validated. A prospective accuracy and usability study sits outside the scope of this capstone.

Reports and Findings
Report

Baseline Staff Survey Report

Twenty-question survey of the general nephrology team (n = 18: 12 attending nephrologists and 6 fellows). 72.2% had never ordered a genetic test directly and 66.7% were unfamiliar with the ordering process. Test selection ranked as the hardest step. This is baseline data only. No survey was run afterwards.

Report

PDSA Cycle 1 Feasibility Report

What happened when the package was taken into clinic. The step that stopped the pathway was the Out-of-Province Panels paperwork: roughly nine minutes per patient, outside the clinic visit, and not something the clinician could hand to anyone else. No direct orders were placed.
