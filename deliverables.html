Project Deliverables

Digital Tools & Infrastructure
Digital Tool

Kidney Genetic Testing Clinical Decision Support App

Web-based tool that determines patient eligibility for genetic testing and recommends the appropriate test type. Clinical logic and rules engine designed by the student; built with an external development team. Built and functioning; internally alpha-tested against real cases and informally reviewed by the field mentor. Not formally validated — a prospective accuracy and usability study sits outside the scope of this capstone.
